bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): the scariest horror story that fits in a single
sentence under 200 characters, judged blind and pairwise by a house judge. Top 10 take a
share of the $5 reward.

## The line

> The tattoo artist finished my sleeve, and the portrait on my arm opened its eyes.

One sentence, 81 characters. An ordinary tattoo appointment turns on a comma: the portrait
inked into the narrator's own skin opens its eyes, and nothing is explained. The horror
cannot be left behind, locked out, or taken off — the reader has to supply what happens next,
and that dread is the whole entry. At 81 bytes it also wins any tie on text size.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, no dependencies, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`
and checks it against the bounty gate first: UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, exactly one
sentence.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
